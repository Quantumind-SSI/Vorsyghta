## Email Body

```
Hi {First Name},

I'm {Your Name} from Ciicerone. We've built a multi-team, fully
agentic cybersecurity threat simulation platform, and we're
selecting a small cohort of financial institutions — traditional
banks and blockchain firms — for a closed pilot programme.

THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING
Financial services remain the most targeted sector for social
engineering, Business Email Compromise (BEC), credential theft,
and supply-chain attacks. Static, once-a-year phishing tests
don't reflect how modern adversaries actually operate, and they
rarely exercise the full detection-and-response chain — from
red team intrusion to blue team detection to compliance
reporting.

WHAT CIICERONE DOES
Ciicerone deploys autonomous AI agents organised into eight
colour teams that simulate, detect, and report on realistic
threat scenarios end-to-end:

  - Red Team    — generates context-aware attacks (phishing,
                  BEC, smishing, supply-chain compromise) using
                  MITRE ATT&CK mapped techniques
  - Blue Team   — behavioural & signature detection, threat
                  hunting, incident response orchestration
  - Purple Team — closes the gap with RLHF-driven after-action
                  reviews and detection validation
  - White Team  — enforces rules of engagement and compliance
                  (GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, HIPAA)
  - Yellow Team — secure build & infrastructure validation
  - Green Team  — remediation tracking and patch verification
  - Orange Team — AI-enriched code review and threat modelling
  - Black Team  — evaluation, benchmarking, and reporting

The platform runs six simulation engine paradigms (Discrete
Event Simulation, Agent-Based Modelling, and more) so scenarios
behave like real adversaries — not canned scripts. It integrates
with Microsoft 365, Proofpoint, KnowBe4, Slack, and SIEM/SOC
tooling, and can run fully offline via local LLMs for
data-sensitive environments.

WHY A PILOT WITH {Company}
We're offering a no-cost, time-boxed pilot (typically 4–6 weeks)
where we'll:

  1. Tailor 3–5 threat scenarios to {Company}'s environment and
     threat profile (e.g. wire-fraud BEC, custodian compromise,
     smart-contract supply-chain, insider exfiltration)
  2. Run agentic red-team simulations against agreed scopes
  3. Measure your blue-team detection and response in real time
  4. Deliver a Purple Team after-action report with prioritised
     remediation and a compliance-ready audit trail

You keep the scenarios, the detection gaps we surface, and the
benchmark report — regardless of whether we continue past the
pilot.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
  - A named security or risk lead as pilot sponsor
  - Read-only access to relevant logs / SIEM for detection
    validation (scope agreed upfront)
  - A short scoping call (30 minutes)

If this is of interest, reply with a couple of times that work
for a 30-minute call, or forward this to the right person on
your team. Happy to share the platform architecture and a sample
after-action report beforehand.

Thanks for your time,

{Your Name}
{Title}, Ciicerone
{Email} | {Phone}
https://github.com/ciicerone/ciicerone

P.S. We can run the pilot entirely on your infrastructure with
local models — no data leaves your environment if that's a
constraint.
```
